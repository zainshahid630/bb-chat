## 💡 **Pro Tips**

### **Reduce OTP Costs:**

1. **Cache OTP for 5 minutes:** Don't send new OTP if user requests again within 5 min
2. **Rate limit:** Max 3 OTPs per number per hour
3. **Use 4-digit OTP:** Shorter SMS = cheaper (if provider charges by length)
4. **Batch verification:** Verify multiple users at once
5. **Email OTP for existing users:** Free alternative

### **Improve Delivery:**

1. **Use DLT registered templates:** Better delivery rates
2. **Send during business hours:** Better delivery (9 AM - 9 PM)
3. **Avoid promotional route:** Use transactional route for OTP
4. **Monitor delivery reports:** Track failed deliveries
5. **Implement fallback:** Voice OTP if SMS fails
